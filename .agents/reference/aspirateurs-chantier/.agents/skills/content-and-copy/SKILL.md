# Content and Copy

Write or edit content that serves a specific reader and goal. This skill owns the prose; specialist skills own SEO, evidence, review methodology and factual verification.

## Five dimensions

### Hook
Open with the decision, tension or specific value. Avoid throat-clearing and vague promises.

### Structure
Choose the structure that best serves this piece. Comparison content may use compare/contrast/recommend, question/answer/implication, decision-tree logic or another justified form. Do not force a reusable skeleton.

### Voice
Keep a calm, precise editorial voice. Read like a specialist publication, not a merchant page or generic AI listicle.

### Substance
Every paragraph should add a fact, distinction, trade-off, implication or useful next step. Cut padding and repeated summaries. Prefer honest limitations and concrete scenarios.

### Closing
End when the decision is resolved. Do not add a generic summary or CTA merely because a template normally has one.

## Workflow for new pieces

1. Confirm brief, evidence, decision and constraints.
2. Outline from the brief and evidence.
3. Draft for substance first.
4. Edit structure before sentence polish.
5. Edit voice and clarity.
6. Run specialist fact-check/SEO/anti-slop skills.

## Workflow for existing content

1. Read the whole piece.
2. Identify and preserve what works.
3. Fix structure before adding prose.
4. Strengthen substance; do not pad.
5. Edit voice and clarity last.

## Failure patterns

- writing before brief/evidence is ready;
- burying the decision;
- generic voice;
- padding to a target length;
- mechanically symmetric product sections;
- repeated conclusions;
- rewriting strong material solely for novelty.
