# Evidence-Based Reviews

Use whenever a judgment goes beyond simple specifications.

## Central rule
Never claim practical experience that did not happen. Desk research can be valuable when it verifies facts, triangulates sources and explains consequences honestly.

## Evidence tiers
- Tier 1: verified manufacturer specifications and official documentation.
- Tier 2: user-experience synthesis from a credible corpus; preserve disagreement and corpus limits.
- Tier 3: triangulation of named independent expert tests; show convergence and divergence.
- Tier 4: genuine hands-on evidence with traceable device/product, duration, conditions, measurements, photos or notes.

A Tier 1 specification does not prove comfort, reliability or superiority. Tier 2/3 observations never become “we observed”.

## Evidence ledger
| Claim / question | Tier | Source | Status | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Use `VERIFIED`, `SUPPORTED`, `INFERRED`, `UNKNOWN`, `OUTDATED`, `CONTRADICTED`.

## Review value
Create a synthesis no single source provides: practical consequences of specs, generation differences, compatibility, total cost, contradictions, documented user patterns, decision criteria and rational alternatives.

FAIL on fake hands-on language, isolated anecdotes presented as trends, anonymous expert claims, unsupported performance judgments, recommendation without criteria, or evidence gaps filled by assumption.
