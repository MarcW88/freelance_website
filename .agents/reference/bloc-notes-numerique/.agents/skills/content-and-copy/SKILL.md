# Content and Copy

Write or edit content that serves a specific reader and goal. This skill owns the actual prose; specialist skills own SEO, evidence, review methodology and factual verification.

## Five dimensions

### 1. Hook
Open with the decision, tension or specific value. Avoid throat-clearing, dictionary definitions and vague promises.

### 2. Structure
Choose the structure that best serves this piece. Comparison content may use compare/contrast/recommend, question/answer/implication, decision-tree logic or another justified form. Do not force a reusable article skeleton.

### 3. Voice
Keep the site's calm, precise editorial voice. Read like a specialist publication, not a merchant page, SaaS landing page or generic AI listicle.

### 4. Substance
Every paragraph should add a fact, distinction, trade-off, implication or useful next step. Cut padding and repeated summaries. Prefer honest limitations and concrete scenarios.

### 5. Closing
End when the decision is resolved. Do not add a generic summary or CTA merely because an article template normally has one.

## Workflow for new pieces

1. Confirm the brief: reader, goal, evidence, decision and constraints.
2. Outline from the brief and evidence.
3. Draft for substance first.
4. Edit structure before sentence polish.
5. Edit for voice and clarity.
6. Run specialist fact-check/SEO/anti-slop skills after drafting.

## Workflow for existing content

1. Read the whole piece before editing.
2. Identify what already works and preserve it.
3. Fix structure before adding prose.
4. Strengthen substance; do not solve weak content by padding.
5. Edit voice and clarity last.

## Failure patterns

- writing before the brief/evidence is ready;
- burying the decision;
- generic voice;
- padding to a target length;
- mechanically symmetric product sections;
- weak transitions and repeated conclusion language;
- rewriting strong existing material solely for novelty.
